## Our Sponsorship & Sustainability Philosophy

The goal of **CodingDatafy** is to build the largest, neutral, and completely free programming content reference for everyone, everywhere, and at all times.

To ensure a strict focus on privacy, the website is built entirely free of annoying, distracting ads. While this project is strictly non-commercial, it does not mean it cannot be self-sustaining. Any generated revenue is directed exclusively toward the platform itself—supporting full-time dedication to developing, maintaining, and managing community contributions on GitHub.

To maintain this vision without compromising our core principles, we have limited our funding methods to two channels that fully respect open-source philosophy: **Direct Donations** and **Privacy-First Direct Sponsorships**, completely free of third-party trackers or ad networks.

> **A Final Critical Note:** Neither sponsors nor donors will ever influence the neutral, free nature of our content, nor the independent direction of CodingDatafy.

---

## AI-First & Sustainable Partnerships

Because CodingDatafy is built from the ground up for an AI-first era, our structured MD/JSON content is optimized for both human developers and AI models. To ensure long-term self-sustainability, we may establish official partnerships or data-licensing agreements with AI providers.

However, any such agreement comes with strict, non-negotiable conditions: **the content must remain 100% free for everyone, forever, and strictly bound by the CC BY-SA 4.0 license.** No AI model will ever lock behind a paywall the knowledge built by our community.

## How to Support Us

Whether you are an individual developer or an organization sharing our commitment to privacy and open-source knowledge, you can support CodingDatafy through:

1. **Direct Donations:** One-time or recurring contributions from individual developers and privacy advocates.
2. **Direct Sponsorships:** For privacy-conscious organizations wishing to align with our mission without compromising user privacy.

To discuss direct sponsorship opportunities or partnerships, please contact us directly at: **contact@codingdatafy.com**
